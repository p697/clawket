## Purchase semantics

Completed purchase-status pages (verification pending, scheduled changes, and lifetime renewal guidance) separate Manage subscription and Done by 16 points, retaining primary/secondary emphasis and the safe-area inset.

- Explicit member navigation is separate from automatic launch/feature gates. The latter still return access immediately for Pro users. Members see their owned plan (marked `Current plan`, locked, raised on `surface` and never dimmed) plus every plan they can still move to; locked alternatives are omitted rather than greyed, so a lifetime owner sees one card. A purchasable alternative is initially selected where available. When the selection is the owned plan there is nothing to buy, so no checkout button or billing caption is rendered and store management is the only action; failure notices still render. If the owned plan cannot be matched to the catalog, the full locked catalog is shown as before.
- Refresh CustomerInfo before opening native checkout. Reject owned plans, foreign-store subscription changes, ambiguous recurring identity and family-shared recurring changes. No optimistic entitlement grant.
- Compare Android product plus base-plan identity. Within one subscription Google permits `WITHOUT_PRORATION` or `CHARGE_FULL_PRICE`; this app chooses the former (SDK 9.11.2 name `PRORATION_MODE.IMMEDIATE_WITHOUT_PRORATION`, value 3), with the old product's subscription ID. The new plan can become active immediately with charging at the next billing date. Across different subscription IDs use `PRORATION_MODE.DEFERRED` (value 6). Do not apply `DEFERRED` to the live same-product monthly/yearly catalog.
- Apple same-group, same-level changes between different durations take effect at renewal. The app presents store-confirmed timing rather than promising an immediate annual charge or a prorated refund.
- Internal purchase outcome `scheduled` means a successful recurring plan-change submission (including Google's next-billing-date mode), not revenue realization. Persist the returned actual entitlement. A store success retaining the old plan is not a new annual entitlement. The installed CustomerInfo API does not expose a reliable pending renewal product; pending changes are reviewed in the store, not invented from local state.
- Lifetime requires the selected product to be the active non-expiring entitlement. Historical non-subscription transactions alone do not prove current ownership after refunds. Lifetime checkout never carries a subscription replacement parameter.
- Preserve per-subscription renewal data separately from the winning entitlement. Display the original-subscription renewal warning before lifetime checkout and a persistent management completion screen afterwards. Buying lifetime does not cancel/refund the old subscription. Management opens RevenueCat's `managementURL` with official store fallback. Foreground refresh reconciles changes after returning.
- Cancellation is silent; pending payments and unconfirmed target access are not successes. Plan-change and lifetime-management completion screens remain dismissible and never run a stale feature continuation. Ordinary new Pro activation keeps the existing two-second success flow.

## Analytics

`paywall_plan_change_submitted` carries the existing package summary and paywall context; it is not an immediate-revenue event. `paywall_manage_subscription_tapped` records the store-management action. `paywall_purchase_succeeded` is reserved for verified activation. Both new events use the central privacy whitelist; no receipts, transaction IDs, purchase tokens or management URLs are captured. PostHog requires no preregistration to ingest them. Actual revenue must be reconciled with store/RevenueCat transactions.
